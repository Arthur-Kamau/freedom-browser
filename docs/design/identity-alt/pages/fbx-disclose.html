Answer a request someone sent you

Paste a disclosure request you received as text, for example in a chat or an email. You will see who asks, what for, and exactly what your answer would contain before anything is signed.

Sites ask differently

A site asks through its wallet connection, and the question opens in the Freedom sidebar next to the site. You answer it there.

Every request is decided fresh. Freedom keeps no list of what each site received and remembers no answers.
