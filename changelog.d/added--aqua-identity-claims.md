- Identity claims: sign statements about yourself that a site or another person can verify, at `freedom://identity`, reached from the Identity button beside the wallet button or the Identity entry in the menu ([Arthur-Kamau/freedom-browser#1](https://github.com/Arthur-Kamau/freedom-browser/pull/1))
  - A name, email, phone or domain claim is an Aqua tree built by the identity service and signed by your wallet on the sidebar's Sign Message screen; email and phone claims need the one-time code the service sends, and come back with the service's own attestation
  - Every claim is verified and kept in this profile, encrypted with a key from your wallet seed, so it is readable only while the wallet is unlocked; the identity service keeps its copy
  - A claim you alone have signed is shown as Self-signed, never as verified; the service you use is a per-profile setting (`aquaNodeUrl`) and defaults to dev.aquafire.inblock.io
  - Attestation by another person: share a claim with their wallet on the identity service, and they attest it under "Attest others" with their own wallet and words; each shared claim there is one card, saying when it was last shared and, once attested, carrying the attestations they issued for it (words, state, hash, Copy JSON) instead of offering Attest again. The attester's Freedom shares the attestation back with the claim's owner on the identity service (also for ones issued earlier, on the next visit), and the owner's Freedom pulls every claim's attestations each time the claims open, imports what was shared back, verifies it against the claim and lists it with the attester's DID; an attestation handed over as a file or pasted JSON is imported the same way, and a failed verification says the verifier's reason
  - A service without email or phone verification (a bare Aqua node) is detected and those claim kinds are withheld rather than failing later
  - A claim is held by the service it was made on: after the identity service address changes, such a claim says so on its card and is neither shared, pulled nor DNS-checked through the new one
  - Settings → Identity holds the identity service address (a new address is saved only once an Aqua identity service answers there), and Export all / Remove all for the claims kept in this profile; `freedom://identity` signs in to the service with one wallet signature before it shows anything that needs a session, and says when the service does not answer, offering that setting
