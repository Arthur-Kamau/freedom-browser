- `freedom://identity` is a record you can check, laid out for a first visit rather than for a verifier
  - The page speaks three words instead of a glossary: a _claim_ is something you say about yourself and sign, it is _verified_ once the node or a person who knows you has signed it too, and asking a person to do that is asking them to _vouch_. "Attestation", DIDs, revision hashes and the chain check live under Technical details and in All details
  - An identity bar leads: your wallet address with a Copy button, and a node card that names the identity service your claims live on, says whether it answers and whether you are signed in, and lists the checks it can run; three tabs, Me, Vouches I give and Requests, replace the left nav
  - Me is a list of your claims beside the selected claim's panel. Each row carries two marks, the node's check (a shield that is teal when checked, amber while pending, red when failed, and quiet for a name, which no node can check) and the people who vouch (one green pip each); a claim that does not verify shows one red mark instead, and its panel says what failed and offers Check again or Remove
  - The panel shows the same two marks as tiles, one plain line that says what to do next, the evidence newest first (every vouch and the node's check as a timeline that ends with your own signature), and Technical details; a domain's TXT record, with Copy on each field and the outcome of the last check, sits in the panel instead of a dialog
  - One readiness checklist (a wallet, unlocked, a node that answers, signed in) replaces the four gate cards: it ticks what is done and shows the one thing to do next with its button; the node card says the same in a word
  - Add a claim opens a composer in place of the list, with the kinds on the left saying how each gets checked, the form on the right, a preview of how the claim will look to a site, and the one-time code for email and phone as three numbered steps (send, enter, sign); the kinds a node cannot check are greyed and say so
  - Asking someone to vouch and importing a vouch are views in the claim panel, with the claim quoted; Remove is reached from All details and says what goes and what stays
  - "Vouch for others" is "Vouches I give": the requests are rows beside a panel that quotes the claim, and the form to vouch (your words, Sign and vouch) sits in the panel; once you have signed, the panel shows what you signed with Copy as file
  - Requests pastes a request on the left and shows the exact response beside it once you have decided in the sidebar, with Copy the response
